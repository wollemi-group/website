About Wollemi Group

Wollemi Group does building, building surveying and civil works, from Maribyrnong in Melbourne's west.

Why Wollemi

The Wollemi pine was known only from fossils millions of years old. In 1994 a national parks officer, David Noble, found a stand of living trees in a canyon in Wollemi National Park, west of Sydney. In the 2019–20 bushfires, firefighters protected that last wild stand, and most of the mature trees survived.

We named the business after it because it lasts. That's what we want from every job we build.

How we work

From the ground up

Building and civil works, including stormwater, drainage and concrete, so jobs are planned from the ground up.

We know the rules

A registered building surveyor on the team. On our own builds, the design meets the rules before the plans go to the independent surveyor. On other people's jobs, we issue the permit.

Built for everyone

Accessible design is part of how we build, from an NDIS bathroom conversion to a stepless front entry.

Who we are

Helen Pham

Co-founder and Director, Compliance and Operations

Helen co-founded Wollemi Group with her husband and is a director of the company. She is a registered building surveyor and a registered domestic builder. Her work in building regulation started in 2006 at the Building Commission, including as a technical adviser to the Building Appeals Board. Since then she has been an assistant building surveyor at Banyule City Council, a director and building surveyor from 2013 to 2021, a building surveyor at Moonee Valley City Council, and an access consultant.

At Wollemi she coordinates projects and manages compliance documents and approval submissions. She is a member of Moonee Valley City Council's Disability Advisory Committee.

Ben

Co-founder

Ben co-founded Wollemi Group with his wife, Helen. He worked for years in IT, managing teams and systems, and spent his weekends on renovations, extensions and townhouse builds. He now works in the business full-time, running sites and schedules, and uses digital tools to help keep jobs on track.
