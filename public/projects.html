Projects

Our work

Townhouses, a granny flat, an extension, a balcony, and civil and concrete work. All photos are from Wollemi jobs.

NDIS bathroom renovation

A bathroom rebuilt for an NDIS participant: stripped out, waterproofed, retiled, with grab rails in the shower.

Townhouses: Albion, Maribyrnong and Essendon

Three townhouse developments of three homes each: double-storey in Albion and Maribyrnong, triple-storey in Essendon. The Albion townhouses were completed in about six months.

Granny flat, St Albans

A small second dwelling in a back yard: steel frame, brick, its own kitchen, bathroom and laundry.

House extension and renovation, West Footscray

An extension and renovation in progress: new stumps, a new floor frame and new roof trusses over the old house.

Balcony repair, Maribyrnong

A first-floor balcony rebuilt: new waterproofing membrane, then large-format tiles.

Balcony repair, Aberfeldie

Another balcony repair: large-format tiles laid on pedestals over the waterproofed floor.

Vehicle turntables, Kew and Brighton

Two driveway turntables: the ring marked out on a finished asphalt drive in Kew, and a concrete drive in Brighton from the steel turntable plate to the finished surface.

Medical clinic, St Albans

A medical clinic we fitted out, with the ramp and landscaping at the front: reception, a corridor and a kitchenette inside, and an accessible ramp with handrails and a landscaped path outside.
