Building maintenance

Property maintenance across Melbourne

Repairs and upkeep for homeowners, landlords and real estate property managers. From a sticking door to a roof leak, and the fixes needed to turn a rental around.

What we cover

Domestic and commercial maintenance. We fix the cause of the problem, not just the mark it leaves.

General repairs and carpentry

Sticking doors, door handles, hinges and locks, latch adjustments, skirtings and architraves, plaster patching and minor framing repairs.

Plumbing and wet areas

Toilet suite repairs and replacements, tapware, leak repairs, silicone and joint sealing, and vanity adjustments.

Roofing and rainwater

Finding and fixing roof leaks, tile replacement, flashing repairs, gutter cleaning, downpipe clearing and minor roof plumbing.

Painting and finishes

Interior and exterior touch-ups, patch repairs, end-of-lease repaints, stain matching and weatherproof sealing.

External works

Deck repairs and re-oiling, step replacement, fence and gate repairs, boundary adjustments and minor concrete and paving.

Turnaround and compliance

Property condition fixes, pre-sale tune-ups, end-of-lease repairs, and safety fixes such as latches, rails and barrier adjustments.

Extensions and major structural work are separate jobs. See our Building page.

Who it's for

Real estate agents and property managers

Quick turnaround on routine maintenance, urgent tenant-reported issues and repairs needed to get a bond back.

Landlords and commercial owners

Ongoing upkeep, minor capital works, preventative maintenance and the repairs the rules require.

Homeowners, strata and body corporates

One-off repairs, upgrades, common-property fixes in apartments and seasonal upkeep.

Where we work

Melbourne's northern, western and inner eastern suburbs.

Urgent call-outs

Scheduled maintenance, plus priority call-outs for problems that can't wait: an active roof leak, a broken lock, an urgent plumbing fault or a tenant security issue.

Working with agents

  • Clear, prompt communication
  • Itemised invoices
  • Before and after photos of every job
  • We coordinate with the tenant directly
