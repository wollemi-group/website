Privacy

Privacy policy

How Wollemi Group Pty Ltd (ABN 17 684 114 529) handles the personal information you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you type into the form: your name, phone number, email address, the suburb of the job, the type of work, whether you are the owner, builder or designer, and your message. If you call or email us, we keep the details you give us.

Why we collect it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, to arrange a site visit and to prepare a quote. We do not sell your details or use them for anything unrelated to your enquiry.

Who sees it

Your enquiry is emailed to us. The website is hosted on Cloudflare, and the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to stop spam and Resend to send us the email. These services handle your details only to deliver the enquiry to us, and some of their servers are outside Australia.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to deal with it and with any job that follows, and we then delete or de-identify it.

Seeing or correcting your details

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or to correct it, by emailing admin@wollemigroup.co or calling Ben on 0481 008 080.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Talk to us about your project

Book a time for us to see the site, or call Ben on 0481 008 080. Send plans or photos with your enquiry and we'll come back to you with the next step.
